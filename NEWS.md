## The six stories

### 1. Russia Strikes, Missiles Land in Poland
Russia fired 400+ drones and 110 missiles at Ukraine overnight; two
Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland. PM Tusk
calls it a deliberate provocation; NATO calls it a grave violation and
moves fighter jets.

### 2. S&P 500 and Dow Enter Correction
US stocks fell 3.7% on Friday as an AI sell-off pushed the S&P 500 and
Dow into correction; the Nasdaq erased its 2026 gains. Nvidia fell about
14% after a report the SEC is examining its accounting.

### 3. Magnitude 6.8 Quake Shakes Peru
A 6.8 earthquake struck the Junín region of central Peru; tremors felt
in Lima, 320 km away. No immediate reports of casualties or major
damage; no tsunami threat.

### 4. Record Foreign Cash Pours Into India
Foreign investors put a net 1.22 lakh crore rupees into Indian shares in
November, the highest monthly inflow on record, ending a 16-month
selling streak.

### 5. Mamdani Sets 2032 to Close Rikers
New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani unveils a plan to close the Rikers Island
jail complex by 2032, replacing it with four smaller borough jails; the
jail population would fall from about 6,600 to at most 4,100.

### 6. KL Rahul Completes the SENA Set
KL Rahul scores 100 in Wellington, becoming the first Indian opener with
Test centuries in all four SENA countries: South Africa, England, New
Zealand and Australia.
